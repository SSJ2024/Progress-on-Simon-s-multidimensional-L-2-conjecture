# Literature status and claim boundary

Checked on 24 July 2026.

Barry Simon formulates the multidimensional \(L^2\) conjecture as follows:
for \(d\geq2\), a potential satisfying the weighted \(L^2\) condition should
leave absolutely continuous spectrum of infinite multiplicity on the positive
half-line. Simon's 2019 discussion states that the problem had resisted
solution, while Denisov's survey describes the known partial results and the
multichannel WKB difficulty.

Primary entry points:

- B. Simon, *Tosio Kato's work on non-relativistic quantum mechanics,
  Part 2*, Conjecture 20.2:
  https://math.caltech.edu/SimonPapers/R62.pdf
- S. A. Denisov, *Multidimensional \(L^2\) conjecture: a survey*:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1203.4255
- P. Deift and R. Killip, *On the absolutely continuous spectrum of
  one-dimensional Schrödinger operators with square summable potentials*:
  https://doi.org/10.1007/s002200050615

The present repository establishes neither the conjectured absolutely
continuous multiplicity nor its failure. Paper I invalidates a fixed-probe
entropy route. Paper II supplies unconditional operator-valued contraction
and no-go theorems but leaves the physical radialization step open.

No public abstract, press description, repository title, or journal cover
letter should describe these manuscripts as a solution of Simon's conjecture
unless the remaining theorem is independently proved and the full spectral
bridge is re-audited.
