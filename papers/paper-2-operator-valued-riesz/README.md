# Paper II

**Simon's Multidimensional \(L^2\) Conjecture: Operator-Valued Reciprocal
Riesz Contraction and the Radial Short-Time Problem**

Compile `main.tex` with Tectonic or a standard LaTeX installation.

The operator-valued contraction and stated no-go results are unconditional.
The full physical contraction is conditional on the explicitly stated
short-angular-time radialization problem. The manuscript does not claim a
counterexample to Simon's conjecture.

The distributed PDF was built with TeX Live 2026.
